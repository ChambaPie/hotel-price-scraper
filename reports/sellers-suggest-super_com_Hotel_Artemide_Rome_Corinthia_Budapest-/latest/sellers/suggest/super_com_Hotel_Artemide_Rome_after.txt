Description
Hotel Artemide sits in a 19th-century building on Via Nazionale, placing you in the heart of Rome and within easy reach of the city's landmarks. The Trevi Fountain is about 1 km away, and both the Spanish Steps and the Roman Forum are roughly 1.4 km out. History is close at hand, with the Baths of Diocletian around 0.7 km away, Palazzo Barberini about 0.5 km, and Quirinale Palace roughly 0.7 km from the door. For culture, Teatro dell'Opera di Roma is about 0.3 km away, while Basilica di Santa Maria Maggiore sits roughly 0.7 km out. The hotel is also just 215 yards from the Repubblica Metro Station, two stops from the Spanish Steps, so getting around the city is straightforward.

Contemporary, air-conditioned rooms come with free minibar items, Smart TVs, premium bedding on memory foam beds, safes, desks, and tea and coffee-making facilities. Free WiFi keeps you connected throughout your stay. On-site, the Ambrosia Rooftop Restaurant & Bar serves Italian cuisine with panoramic views over Rome, along with a bar and lounge and alfresco dining. A buffet breakfast is available for a fee, and room service is offered during limited hours. Wind down at the full-service Artemis Spa, which offers massages, body treatments, facials, a sauna, a hot tub, and a Turkish bath, and keep up your routine at the 24-hour fitness center.

Practical touches include a business center with limo and town car service, free newspapers in the lobby, and a 24-hour front desk. Airport shuttle service to and from Fiumicino and Ciampino airports is available for a surcharge. The property is smoke-free, offers contactless check-in and check-out, and supports cashless payment. Eco-conscious travelers will appreciate the hotel's 100% renewable electricity, LED lighting, vegetable garden, living plant wall, and use of locally-sourced and organic food.
