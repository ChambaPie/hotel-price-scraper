Hotel Don Pepe Gran Meliá

For generations, this luxury hotel in Marbella has been one of the great benchmarks on the Costa del Sol. Located on the prestigious Golden Mile, facing the Mediterranean Sea, this iconic retreat has been redefined to offer an even more exclusive experience, keeping intact the essence and the bond with the destination that has given it its identity over time.

Learn more
Meliá Cala Millor

Discover the quieter side of Mallorca at this 4-star hotel located on the beachfront of Cala Millor, on the east coast of the island and next to the protected natural area of Punta de n'Amer. Surrender to the union of sea, wellness and nature in a privileged setting. Wake up to spectacular views of the Mediterranean and stroll along the shore of one of the island's most appreciated beaches.

Learn more
Casa Azure Meliá Collection

Bathed in the golden light of the Mediterranean, this 4-star boutique hotel in Gozo blends naturally and harmoniously into this island northwest of Malta, where history and landscape coexist in harmony. Ancient stone villages like San Lawrenz, incredible cliffs in Dwejra, and discovering crystal-clear coves in Xlendi are all part of the charm of your stay. An experience that calms and awakens the soul.

Learn more
Palazzo Giuseppe Meliá Collection

Arriving at this boutique hotel in Gozo is like stepping into a natural paradise surrounded by the blue sea, sharp-edged cliffs, crystal-clear waters in its coves, and a mystical atmosphere. The capital Victoria, also known as Rabat, reveals a magical medieval world in its citadel. An authentic connection with history and that warm, familiar feeling of home.

Learn more
Casa Alameda 1700 Meliá Collection

The iconic Alameda Apodaca is graced by the imposing presence of this five-star boutique hotel. With the Atlantic as a backdrop, it invites you to discover a new way to experience Cadiz. Located in a historic 1700 palace house, this exclusive luxury retreat combines the charm of its architectural heritage with a contemporary vision of Wellness, design, and hospitality..

Learn more
INNSiDE Mexico Roma Norte

You don't need a plan to let yourself be carried away by Roma Norte, a neighborhood that invites you to discover even the smallest detail. From here, the city will be yours: walk to Condesa, explore Reforma, and let yourself be surprised by every café, bar, and gallery you find.

Learn more
Residenza del Cardinale Meliá Collection

In the historic heart of Milan, where every street whispers centuries of art and creativity, this refuge with a soul of its own emerges, where Milanese elegance flows naturally. Its privileged location, just a few steps from the Duomo and the Pinacoteca Ambrosiana, invites you to experience the city from an authentic perspective, between the cultural heritage that defines it and the contemporary energy that drives it.

Learn more
Hacienda del Mar Meliá Collection

Enjoy the essence of southern Spain in this emblematic beachfront hotel in Estepona. Discover the heritage of past civilisations in a prime location near Marbella, where the history and beauty of the Costa del Sol merge. The most exotic Mediterranean is present in lush gardens, bright suites and rooms, and an exclusive wellness area with a spa and outdoor pools. An oasis of calm, authenticity, and elegance.

Learn more
Hotel Ben Marsalforn Affiliated by Meliá

The perfect base for exploring an island that invites relaxation and enjoyment of the surroundings.
