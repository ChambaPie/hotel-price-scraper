FAQ
What do you mean when you say that you will make my bookings 'more profitable'?

It means driving down your total Cost Per Acquisition (CPA). Unlike OTAs with fixed commission, our end-to-end integrated strategy boosts high-margin, direct bookings, lowering your costs.

How does the 'partnership' with a Client Success Manager (CSM) work?

Your dedicated CSM is your direct booking expert. They work with you to set and achieve strategic goals, coordinate our teams, and ensure you get the most from our technology and services.

How does Bookassist use Artificial Intelligence (AI)?

We integrate AI to deliver real value, not hype. AI powers marketing decisions, content generation, and personalised offers in our Booking Platform to measurably enhance conversion.

Do you only work with large hotel groups?

We partner with hundreds of hotels worldwide, from large, prestigious groups to independent properties. Our scalable solutions are tailored to meet your specific requirements.

Can your services work with my existing website or booking engine?

Yes. While our ecosystem is most powerful when fully integrated, our services are designed to be flexible. Our Digital Media services, for example, can operate with any website or booking engine.
